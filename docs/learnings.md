# What we've learned

Evidence and feedback that should change how the next batch is written.
Separate from `style.md` on purpose: `style.md` holds the settled rules of
how a script sounds, this file holds the changing picture of what's actually
working on this channel.

**Read this before writing any batch.** It is the only thing that carries
between sessions — Claude starts each session with no memory of the last one,
so anything not written here is lost.

---

## Feedback from review sessions

_Each entry: what was said, and what to do differently. Add to this every
time a script or idea gets denied._

- **2026-09-15 — thumbnail text was unusable.** On-screen text was being
  machine-generated by taking the first four words of the narration, which
  produced single disconnected words like "phone". Fixed in code (it is now
  written deliberately per script and validated to be 2-6 words), and the
  rules are in `style.md` under "The opening frame IS the thumbnail".
  Worked example given: for the T-Rex / Stegosaurus time-gap fact, the right
  text is "THE T-REX TIME ILLUSION" and the right opening image is the two
  dinosaurs facing each other — not a phone on a table, which was a prop
  mentioned in the script rather than the idea of the video.

## What the numbers say

_Updated each month from `data/performance_log.json` and the weekly report._

- **Views are stuck around 1,000-1,300.** As of 2026-09-08, across the first
  10 videos with data after the space-niche pivot: max 1,233, median 1,097,
  none above 1,300. The pivot lifted the floor (median rose from ~906) but
  has not broken the ceiling, which was the entire point of it. Retention is
  fine (51-103%), so the problem is not that people dislike the videos — it
  is that not enough people are being shown them.
- **One video collapsed to 29 views** (2026-09-04, "Uranus Really Does Smell
  Like Rotten Eggs") while its neighbours did 871-1,233. Still unexplained.
  Worth checking in YouTube Studio whether it was flagged, mis-categorised,
  or restricted.

## Open questions

- Is the ~1,300 ceiling a content problem or a format problem? Thirty-seven
  videos in, the shape of the data increasingly suggests the format (AI voice,
  synthetic visuals, 45 seconds) rather than the topics.
- We cannot yet tell whether viewers leave at second 3 or second 25, because
  only average retention is collected, not the per-second curve. Until that
  changes, script fixes are guesswork about which part is weak.
